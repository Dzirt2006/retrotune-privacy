# RetroTune Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 28, 2026

RetroTune is a music player for the music files stored on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, what leaves your device, and why.

**In short:** RetroTune has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run a server and does not receive any of your data. A few optional features send song details to free music services, described below.

## What stays on your device

- **Your music files.** RetroTune reads the audio files on your device (permission: *Music and audio* / *Storage*) to build your library and play them. They are never uploaded.
- **Your library data.** Playlists, favorites, play counts, settings, queue and edited song details are stored only on your device.
- **Backups.** If you create a backup, it is saved to a file and location you choose. RetroTune does not upload it anywhere.
- **Microphone permission.** Android requires the *Microphone* permission for the optional real-time visualizer, which reads the sound RetroTune itself is playing to animate the bars. **RetroTune never records, stores or sends audio from your microphone.** If you decline, a visualizer that needs no permission is used instead.
- **Notifications.** Used only to show playback controls while music plays.

## What is sent over the internet, and to whom

RetroTune contacts these services directly from your device. The developer never sees these requests.

| Feature | Service | What is sent | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lyrics | [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) | Song title, artist, album and duration | When lyrics are shown for a song |
| Album artwork | [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) and [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org) | Artist and album name | When artwork is missing for an album |
| Scrobbling (optional) | [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm) | Your Last.fm username and password (once, to sign in), then the songs you play and when | Only if you sign in to Last.fm in Settings |
| Scrobbling (optional) | [ListenBrainz](https://listenbrainz.org) | Your ListenBrainz token, then the songs you play and when | Only if you connect ListenBrainz in Settings |

- **Last.fm sign-in:** your password is sent only to Last.fm to get a sign-in key. RetroTune keeps that key on your device and does not store your password.
- **Turning scrobbling off:** sign out in Settings, or uninstall the app, to stop scrobbling. Data already sent to Last.fm or ListenBrainz is handled under their own privacy policies, and you can delete it from your account there.
- **Like any internet request,** these services can see your device's IP address.

## What RetroTune does not do

- No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
- No selling or sharing of personal data for advertising.
- No location, contacts, camera or phone data.

## Children

RetroTune is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling RetroTune deletes all of its data from your device. Android's own device backup may keep a copy of app settings in your Google account, which you can manage in your phone's backup settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
